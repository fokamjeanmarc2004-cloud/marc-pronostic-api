Important

Responsible Gambling

Sports betting should remain entertainment, never a source of income nor a solution to financial difficulties. Here are a few essential rules.

The basic rules

  • For adults only (18 and over).
  • Only stake what you can afford to lose.
  • Set yourself a budget and time limits, and stick to them.
  • Never bet to "win back" a loss.
  • Betting is not a way to make money regularly.

Warning signs

  • You bet more than planned, or with money you need.
  • You think about betting constantly.
  • You hide your bets from those close to you.
  • You bet to escape problems or stress.

Need help?

If gambling becomes a problem for you or someone close to you, talk to a trusted person and reach out to a local support organization specialized in gambling addiction. Most bookmakers also offer self-limitation and self-exclusion tools in your account settings.

Gambling should stay fun. If it no longer is, take a break.
